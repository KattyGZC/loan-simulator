Privacy policy

Loan Simulator, a Chrome extension. Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Loan Simulator does not collect, send or share any data. What you type stays in your browser.

What the extension keeps, and where

  • In your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local): the forms you were filling in, the scenarios you save and your preferences (currency, the decimal sign of exported files and how SOLCA starts on a new loan).
  • In the browser's session storage (chrome.storage.session): the table you send to print, until the browser closes.

None of it leaves your computer. The extension has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep the data above.
  • sidePanel: to show the simulator in Chrome's side panel.

It does not ask for access to the websites you visit.

Links you open

An institution's values come with a link to the document they were read from. If you click it, Chrome opens that website in a new tab, and that website's own privacy policy applies. The extension sends it nothing.

Exported files

A CSV file you export is saved where your browser saves downloads. What happens to it after that is up to you.

Deleting your data

  • Start over empties a tab's form.
  • A saved scenario is deleted from the Scenarios tab.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it kept.

Changes

If the extension ever needs to handle data differently, this page will say so before that version is published, and the date above will change.
